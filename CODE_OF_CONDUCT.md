# Code of Conduct

Ambar OS is for everyone. We want the project to be a place where anyone can ask a question, report a problem or contribute without being made to feel small.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Linux or to open source.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Accept that a decision went the other way, and move on.
- Give credit for other people's work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background or ability
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Persistent disruption of discussions

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, chats and events. It also applies when you represent the project elsewhere.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact on the GitHub organisation or profile page, or with GitHub's *Report content* option. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, after a warning where that's appropriate.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
